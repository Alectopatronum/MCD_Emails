About ACSC

Building Sustainable Change Together

ACSC is MEA’s annual two-day event that introduces Grades 9–12 high school students to the principles of the organization’s core advocacy of Corporate Sustainability (CS). Through plenary talks, interactive modules, an Amazing Race, and a culminating activity, the project develops a systems-thinking mindset centered on balancing people, planet, and profit to empower young leaders as catalysts for sustainable change.

Beyond serving participants, ACSC is equally a space to live out these values within its core team — deepening our collective commitment to CS, while strengthening leadership and project management.

“In ACSC, you won’t just learn about CS — you will have the chance to explore what it means, shape how others experience it, and discover how it can take root in your own life. In this year’s ACSC, everyone is invited to bring their own piece to the table, building a shared vision of CS to carry beyond the project.”
